# SOLAR SYSTEM STUDY GUIDE

[CARD 1]
TOPIC: Mercury
FACTS: Closest planet to the Sun; smallest planet in our solar system; extreme temperature swings from minus 180 to 430 degrees Celsius; has almost no atmosphere to trap heat.
KEY_CONCEPT: Closest to the Sun and lacks an atmosphere to retain heat.

[CARD 2]
TOPIC: Venus
FACTS: Second planet from the Sun; hottest planet due to a thick carbon dioxide greenhouse atmosphere; rotates backward in retrograde motion; similar in size to Earth.
KEY_CONCEPT: Runaway greenhouse effect from thick carbon dioxide and retrograde rotation.

[CARD 3]
TOPIC: Earth
FACTS: Third planet from the Sun; only known world with liquid surface water and life; nitrogen and oxygen atmosphere protects the surface; has one large natural moon.
KEY_CONCEPT: Liquid surface water, protective atmosphere, and life.

[CARD 4]
TOPIC: Mars
FACTS: Fourth planet from the Sun; red color comes from iron oxide dust or rust; home to Olympus Mons, the largest volcano in the solar system; has two tiny moons, Phobos and Deimos.
KEY_CONCEPT: Iron oxide rust surface, Olympus Mons volcano, and moons Phobos and Deimos.

[CARD 5]
TOPIC: Jupiter
FACTS: Fifth planet from the Sun and the largest planet; a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium; famous for the Great Red Spot, a giant centuries-old storm; has dozens of moons including Europa and Ganymede.
KEY_CONCEPT: Largest gas giant and the Great Red Spot storm.

[CARD 6]
TOPIC: Saturn
FACTS: Sixth planet from the Sun; gas giant famous for its bright icy ring system; lowest density of any planet, lower than liquid water; largest moon is Titan.
KEY_CONCEPT: Prominent icy rings and lowest planetary density.

[CARD 7]
TOPIC: Uranus
FACTS: Seventh planet from the Sun; an ice giant with a pale cyan color from methane gas; rotates almost completely on its side at a 98-degree tilt.
KEY_CONCEPT: Ice giant colored by methane that spins sideways.

[CARD 8]
TOPIC: Neptune
FACTS: Eighth and farthest recognized planet from the Sun; deep blue ice giant; fastest supersonic winds in the solar system; largest moon is Triton.
KEY_CONCEPT: Farthest planet with the strongest winds in the solar system.
